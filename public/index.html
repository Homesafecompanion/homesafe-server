Peace of mind for families caring at a distance

HomeSafe helps UK families stay close to elderly loved ones without hovering. Two apps that work together: one for the person being cared for, one for the family members looking out for them.

Coming soon to the App Store

How it works

1

Your loved one installs HomeSafe UK

The elderly relative uses HomeSafe UK on their own phone. It records medical details, doctor information, and daily check-ins in a simple, calm interface.

2

They share an 8-digit family code

One code, one family. Everyone who needs to help — spouse, children, grandchildren — joins using the same code from HomeSafe Carer on their own device.

3

You stay informed, quietly

The Carer app shows their status, medical information, address, and doctor at a glance. Call, message, or open WhatsApp with one tap. No dashboards to check, no reports to read.

Who HomeSafe is for

The person being cared for

Older adults who live independently but want their family to have the essentials — who to call in an emergency, what medications they take, which doctor they see — without needing to be asked every week.

The family looking out for them

Adult children, siblings, or partners — often in a different city or country — who want to know the important things are under control and be one tap away when something changes.
